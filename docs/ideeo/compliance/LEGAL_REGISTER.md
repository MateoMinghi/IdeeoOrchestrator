# Legal and regulatory register

Status: **Draft. Every entry is pending legal confirmation.** This register identifies the legal, statutory, regulatory and contractual requirements that apply to the engine (ISO/IEC 27001:2022 control 5.31; ISO/IEC 42001:2023 clause 4.1; ISO/IEC 27701). It is not legal advice. Qualified Mexican counsel must confirm each entry, its citation and its effect before it is relied on for a release. Owner: Legal counsel (to be engaged), with the compliance owner (to be appointed).

Rules for using this register are in [COMPLIANCE.md](../policies/COMPLIANCE.md). Several 2026 instruments below were taken from the [architecture reference](../product/architecture/arquitectura-motor-agentes-rev4.html), section 7.5, which itself marks them "todo por confirmar con legal"; they have not been checked against the Diario Oficial de la Federación (DOF).

## Scope assumptions

- The first tenant (PayMe) operates in Mexico. Whether it is a credit institution (bank) or an electronic payment funds institution (IFPE) is unconfirmed (see Q1). The answer selects the CNBV rulebook for entries LR-03 to LR-05 and LR-09.
- The tenant is the controller (*responsable*) and the regulated entity. We are its technology service provider and processor (*encargado*). See [COMPLIANCE.md](../policies/COMPLIANCE.md#2-roles-under-the-law).
- Most financial regulation binds the tenant and reaches us through its contracts and audit rights. Data-protection, criminal and contractual obligations can bind us directly.
