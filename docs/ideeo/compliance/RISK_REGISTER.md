## Administration development evidence

The new [administration console](../contracts/ADMINISTRATION.md) extends R-02,
R-03/R-04, R-06/R-07, R-09, R-12/R-13, R-14 and R-22. Scoped operator roles,
forced administration RLS, OIDC/PKCE/MFA validation, encrypted transcripts and
secrets, append-only access/change/denial audit and default-denied real-tenant
configuration inference are development controls. Their evidence is linked from
[verification](../reference/development/VERIFICATION.md); they do not close bank-authority
logging, production identity, KMS, contracts or deployment gaps.

R-12/G5 also covers operator retirement/pseudonymization and tenant-wide console
erasure; R-13/G6 covers final audit, replay and configuration-history retention.
Session revocation and expiring transcript cleanup are implemented, while those
broader rights/retention workflows need owner and counsel decisions before
production. No risk score or acceptance is changed here. The console's locked
dependency audit adds a scoped CI control to G8; engine-wide SBOM, scanner,
branch-protection and other existing G8 work remain open.
